# Curriculum gap analysis and educational decisions

**Completed educational proposal for review — 20 October 2026. Not implemented.** Based on selective, documented consultation of all eight supplied books and direct checks of the key visual examples used in this plan. This is not a claim to have read every page, verified every score, heard companion audio, or demonstrated learner outcomes.

## What changes educationally

Recommend **38 Writing lessons, 36 Music lessons and seven optional shared studios**. These are distinct units/projects, not 81 compulsory five-minute sessions. A learner can take either faculty independently. Counts describe introductions and guided revisits; proficiency requires time and evidence beyond completion.

## What source verification changed

The verification register in [LIBRARY_CATALOGUE.md](LIBRARY_CATALOGUE.md) identifies exact pages/assets and bounded claims. Important corrections: IIImin7 versus OCR's IImin7; printed C major versus OCR's G; C6/Amin7 versus prose's plain A minor; missing accidentals in triad extraction; ties versus extra note attacks; and a metric-foot chart whose triple-metre qualifier disappears in OCR. The chord-substitution example changes more than one chord, so it is unsuitable as an unmodified “one variable” exercise. These are reasons to author checked original material, not distribute reconstructed source examples.

Retain conditional artistic interpretations. A major chord need not sound happy, an odd line count need not sound unstable, a ballad need not use one mandatory rhyme scheme, and compound metre need not swing. Prosody can deliberately conflict with literal meaning to create irony. Compare effects against the learner's intended speaker/context.

## Assessment and educational validation

For each faculty stage, collect an initial attempt, a specific revision, the learner's reason and a later unfamiliar attempt. Judge observable criterion evidence separately from subjective effect and actual performance. Track source draft/content version so later feedback is not misapplied. A learner can be “independently demonstrated” on one skill and “introduced” on another; never turn an overall completion percentage into mastery.

A review pilot should include a writing teacher, a songwriting/music educator, a guitarist who reads tabs and learners with differing playing/composition experience. Observe task duration, representation errors and whether they can explain and transfer choices without a recipe. Compare tutor feedback with educator judgment and preserve disagreements. Timing risks remain in Writing 17/22/29/30 and Music 21/32; use existing material and label extended variants. No such learner study has been conducted here.

## Scope decisions

Do not inflate the lesson count to mirror four overlapping music books or the 50/53 Kachulis source lessons. Merge overlapping ideas and revisit them under a new constraint. The extra four Music units compared with the draft make compound grouping, cadence, singer-friendly transposition and bass roles explicit. Keep the seven studios separate and optional. Preserve original drafts, student progress and all 30 deployed lesson IDs. Implement only after review of [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
