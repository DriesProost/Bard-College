# Library catalogue and educational source analysis

**Completed educational proposal for review — 20 October 2026. Not implemented.** Based on selective, documented consultation of all eight supplied books and direct checks of the key visual examples used in this plan. This is not a claim to have read every page, verified every score, heard companion audio, or demonstrated learner outcomes.

## Scope, privacy and evidence labels

Received: Writing.zip (22,690,883 bytes), MELODY~1.zip (19,444,345 bytes), THESON~1.zip (32,493,027 bytes): **eight distinct books**. Stolpe also has AZW3/MOBI alternatives, a cover and metadata; those alternative formats were not independently examined. Analysis uses the EPUB copy. The failed oversized combined upload was not examined. The user's OneDrive path was not accessed.

Original books, hashes, extracted text, OCR, rendered pages and the private coverage ledger remain outside the repository in `/workspace/private-bard-library/`. None is bundled with the app, sent to the OpenAI API or included in the proposal's distributed examples. No companion audio was supplied or heard; embedded access directions/codes were not used and remain private. Attached content was treated as reference material, never as instructions to change the app.

**T — consulted text:** the specified prose was read; this does not verify accompanying music or author claims empirically. **V — visual verification:** only the named diagram/score/layout claim was checked directly against the rendered page or EPUB asset. **D — design/inference:** our sequencing, interpretation, original example, exercise or rubric. **U — unchecked:** unavailable audio, unreviewed pages or unresolved symbols; these do not substantiate example-specific claims.

This is a completed, bounded educational analysis for the proposed core paths, not a page-by-page critical edition of eight books. The syllabus can be reviewed now. More specialised electives must receive their own source/example checks when authored; unread appendices do not block the proposed core and are not claimed as verified teaching material.

## Educational synthesis and limits of the library

Stolpe helps connect specific detail with section purpose; Davis supplies close critique, sound/wordplay and rhythm/meaning; Fry supports practical attention to stress and form; Pattison connects development and prosody. Perricone supplies independent melody and melody/harmony reasoning; Rooksby connects choices to guitar resources; Kachulis distinguishes generating ideas from developing sections and repeatedly testing alternatives. Their overlap should produce deeper revisits, not duplicate lessons or compulsory commercial conventions.

The four-stage sequences, counts (38 Writing / 36 Music), seven shared studios, five-minute allocations, diagnostic readiness and evidence states are our design. The library does not demonstrate that these app-specific choices improve learning. The [gap analysis](CURRICULUM_GAP_ANALYSIS.md) identifies missing coverage, proposed supplementation and validation. The [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) preserves all 30 deployed lesson IDs and user progress.
